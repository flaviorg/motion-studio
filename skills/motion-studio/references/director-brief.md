# Director brief

Use it for client work, videos over ~20 s, or YouTube. Fill it in with the user. Bracketed
fields are what they (or you, if they say "you decide") need to define.

```
Make a [length]-second motion graphics video for [product], with the energy of a motion
designer's showreel. Go all out.

INPUTS
- Product: [name]. Main line: [the site's main headline / the user's line].
- Site: [url] — visit it, capture real screenshots with Playwright, the real logo, colors and
  fonts. Save everything to [folder]/assets and list what you found before animating.
- Existing brand kit: [path to brand.md, if any].
- Reference: [video/link] — use its motion and design, not its content.

STORY
- Hook (0–3 s): [what grabs attention]
- Problem / context: [...]
- Product in action: [which screens/features, in which order]
- Proof / benefit: [...]
- Close: [logo + CTA: "..."]

DIRECTION
- Emotion: [e.g. confident and premium / playful and fast / epic]
- Pacing: [e.g. quick cuts in the hook, breathing room in the middle, crescendo at the end]
- Visual style: [e.g. giant type, real screenshots in mockups, push-in camera]
- Avoid: [e.g. gradients, emoji, particles]

SOUND
- Original soundtrack at [BPM] bpm, mood [...]. SFX on the beat.
- Voiceover: [none / file X / script for TTS].

FORMATS
- [16x9 for YouTube] [9x16 for Reels/TikTok/Shorts] [1x1] [4x5]

PROCESS
- Show me the list of states on the beat grid as a table before writing code.
- When I approve, render, run the critique loop and save to [folder]/render.
```

## Building the DIRECTION section with the user

If they don't know what to put there, ask — ideally one thing at a time:
1. What should the viewer feel at the end? (confidence, excitement, curiosity, urgency)
2. Which video/brand has the look you imagine? (link or description)
3. Faster and intense, or calmer and premium?
4. What is the one thing the viewer must remember?
5. What is the action at the end? (link, follow, free trial)

Then propose a ready-made DIRECTION section and let them adjust it.

Tip: if the user has no story yet, co-write it in a separate conversation first ("help me write
an interesting story for this video"), then paste the result into the brief.
